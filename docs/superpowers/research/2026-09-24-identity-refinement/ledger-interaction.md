## Summary of `recommend` items by felt impact
- **high**: header-nav touch targets (20px tall), theme-swap touch target (15×20px), record-slide motion ignoring `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **med**: focus-ring contrast on `label-night` card/aside surfaces (2.94:1), tab hover/cursor absence, tracklist screen-reader structure, page-level text-selection color.
- **low-med**: header-nav hover feedback, figure cursor affordance.
- **low**: film-edge caption/alt duplication, placeholder contrast on the light theme only.

All items reuse tokens or idioms already present in the sheet (opacity dials, existing outline/box-shadow mechanics, existing color variables); none propose a new color, font, device, or layout change.
