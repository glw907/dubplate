# Artist sweep list

Extrapolated from Geoff's round-one requests (2026-09-25 and 26) across every scene in
`photos/meta/vocab.json`. Older eras rank first for every artist (Geoff: "older is generally
better"); an artist marked *newer ok* takes a recent photo as readily as a period one. Already in the
pool or already searched this round: Fishbone, Bad Manners, the Cramps, Tim Armstrong, Bad Brains,
Minor Threat, Fugazi, the Selecter, Bob Marley, the Fuzztones, the Standells, the Chocolate
Watchband, Q65, Run-DMC, Kool Herc, and the gaps logged in `round-1.md` (the New York Dolls,
Government Issue, Operation Ivy, the Feelies, Yellowman, King Tubby, Nightmares on Wax).

## Reggae

- Ska and rocksteady: the Skatalites, Prince Buster, Desmond Dekker, Toots and the Maytals, Alton
  Ellis, Laurel Aitken, Derrick Morgan, Justin Hinds, the Paragons
- 2 Tone: the Specials, the Beat (English Beat), Madness, the Bodysnatchers, Rico Rodriguez
- Roots and dub: Lee "Scratch" Perry, Augustus Pablo, Burning Spear, Culture, Black Uhuru, Steel
  Pulse, Aswad, Misty in Roots, Linton Kwesi Johnson, Mikey Dread, Scientist, Mad Professor, Jah
  Shaka, Big Youth, U-Roy, I-Roy, the Abyssinians, the Congos, Max Romeo, Horace Andy, Gregory
  Isaacs, Dennis Brown
- Dancehall: Eek-A-Mouse, Barrington Levy, Sugar Minott, Josey Wales, Tenor Saw, Shabba Ranks
- Lovers rock: Janet Kay, Carroll Thompson, Dennis Bovell, Louisa Mark

## Punk

- Proto-punk: the Stooges, MC5, the Velvet Underground, Suicide, the Modern Lovers
- New York punk: Richard Hell, Television, the Ramones, Dead Boys, Blondie, Patti Smith, Johnny
  Thunders and the Heartbreakers
- UK punk and post-punk: the Clash, X-Ray Spex, the Raincoats, Gang of Four, the Pop Group, the
  Ruts, Buzzcocks, Wire, the Adverts
- US hardcore: Rites of Spring, Scream, Void, Black Flag, Minutemen, Hüsker Dü, the Replacements,
  Dead Kennedys, Circle Jerks, Descendents
- Los Angeles punk: X, the Germs, the Weirdos, the Plugz, the Gun Club

## Hip-hop

- Old school: Grandmaster Flash, Afrika Bambaataa, Grandmaster Caz, the Cold Crush Brothers, the
  Treacherous Three, Kurtis Blow, the Sugarhill Gang, Spoonie Gee, Roxanne Shanté, Salt-N-Pepa,
  Boogie Down Productions, Public Enemy, Eric B. & Rakim, Big Daddy Kane, MC Lyte
- Go-go: Chuck Brown, Trouble Funk, Rare Essence, E.U., Junkyard Band, Backyard Band, Little Benny &
  the Masters, Redds and the Boys, Hot Cold Sweat, Sugar Bear; scene (*newer ok*): the Howard Theatre,
  Takoma Station, the Go-Go Museum, Don't Mute DC and Moechella (2019), Chuck Brown Memorial Park
- Graffiti and b-boy: Fab 5 Freddy, Lee Quiñones, Futura, the Rock Steady Crew, Crazy Legs

## Everything else

- Garage: the Sonics, the Seeds, the 13th Floor Elevators, the Monks, ? and the Mysterians, the
  Kingsmen, the Trashmen; revival: the Fleshtones, the Chesterfield Kings, the Lyres, the Miracle
  Workers, the Milkshakes, Billy Childish
- Rockabilly and psychobilly: Bo Diddley, Hasil Adkins, Charlie Feathers, Wanda Jackson, Link Wray, Sleepy
  LaBeef, Ronnie Dawson, the Meteors, the Stray Cats
- Outsider artists: the Shaggs, Daniel Johnston, Wesley Willis, Jandek, Moondog, Wild Man Fischer
- Mod: the Small Faces, the Creation, the Action, the Jam, Secret Affair
- Blues: Howlin' Wolf, Muddy Waters, R. L. Burnside, Junior Kimbrough, Hound Dog Taylor, Big Mama
  Thornton, Lightnin' Hopkins
- Jazz: Sun Ra, Ornette Coleman, Albert Ayler, Mingus, Thelonious Monk, Art Blakey
- Highlife and afrobeat: Fela Kuti, Tony Allen, E. T. Mensah, Ebo Taylor, Orlando Julius, Oriental
  Brothers
- Cumbia and sonidero: Lucho Bermúdez, Andrés Landero, Los Corraleros de Majagual, Sonido La
  Changa, Sonido Pancho
- Rebetiko: Markos Vamvakaris, Rosa Eskenazi, Rita Abatzi, Vassilis Tsitsanis
- Indie and college rock: Yo La Tengo, Galaxie 500, the Embarrassment, Pylon, the dB's, Mission of
  Burma
- Bristol, Warp, and trip-hop (*newer ok*): Massive Attack, Smith & Mighty, Tricky, LFO, Coldcut,
  Nightmares on Wax
