# Reggae/ska/dub period photo search — retry pass (2026-09-24)

Scope: dubplate login-page rotation. Deep cuts favored; at most one Bob Marley
image total (already exists, skip). Period target ~1960-1988, with the
Skatalites exception (owner priority: any year acceptable, reunion-era touring
photos count, but rank 1963-65 first — none of that vintage turned up).
Skip: Commons material on Bob Marley/Selecter Berlin 1980/Bad Brains
1983/Millie Small 1964/Peter Tosh frames, Beth Lesser's reggae-vibes.com
archive, and Commons/LoC/Anefo "in general" (prior pass's territory) — except
where the owner's mid-task Skatalites priority explicitly pointed back at
Commons for that one band, which this pass followed.

## Shippable (PD / CC0 / BY / BY-SA) — everything else searched

**None found beyond the Skatalites four above.** Every other route run this
pass (Openverse, Europeana `reusability=open`, Internet Archive advanced
search, general web search for Flickr pages) returned either no reggae/ska
subject matter, wrong-era content, or unlicensed/rights-reserved material.

## NC/ND (add-on pack only)

None found and verified this pass. Internet Archive's advancedsearch surfaced
scattered CC BY-NC / BY-NC-ND / BY-NC-SA hits for nearly every query, but on
inspection every one was a false keyword match (herbarium specimens named
after "Don" for "Don Drummond," a NASA "Honorary awards ceremony" for "Dennis
Brown," a Puerto Rico boxing schedule for "Rico Rodriguez," etc.) — see next
section. Europeana's `reusability=restricted` runs for "reggae concert,"
"Kingston Jamaica street," "Jamaica market," and "carnival London" returned
nothing period-dated and reggae-relevant either (old nautical charts, a 1907
Kingston earthquake street scene under In Copyright-EDU rather than CC, a
handful of unrelated NC-licensed ephemera). Nothing to log in this class from
this pass.

One near-miss, recorded for completeness though it is a poster, not a
photograph, and therefore out of scope for a full-bleed photo rotation
regardless of license: Regional Archive Nijmegen (Netherlands), via
Europeana, holds a full run of hand-drawn/typeset gig announcements for the
Doornroosje venue's reggae nights, 1979-1990, license CC BY-SA 4.0 quoted
directly from Europeana's `rights` field, e.g. "Roots en Black Slate treden
op in Doornroosje op 5 mei 1979" (1979, confirmed by the item's own `year`
field) — a real 1979 date, real reggae content (Black Slate, a deep-cut UK
reggae band on the owner's list), openly licensed, but it is a paper flyer
scan, not a photograph, so it does not fit "full-bleed period photograph."
Flagging in case the login rotation ever wants a text/ephemera treatment.

## Access routes: what worked, what didn't

1. **Openverse API — blocked, confirmed persistently.** Every attempt across
   three separate tries, spaced roughly 3+ minutes apart (immediate curl,
   curl after a 180s backoff, and a WebFetch attempt as a fourth distinct
   client), returned Cloudflare's interstitial: first two gave HTTP 429 with
   `cf-mitigated: challenge` and a "Just a moment..." challenge page body
   instead of JSON; the WebFetch attempt returned a flat "429 Too Many
   Requests" with no body. Never got past the challenge. Per the retry
   budget (back off 2-5 min, retry up to three times), this route is
   recorded as still blocked and not worth a fourth attempt in this pass.

2. **Europeana Search API (api2demo key) — worked mechanically, unproductive
   for photographs.** Returned real JSON with real `rights` fields for every
   query. `reusability=open` runs for "reggae," "ska jamaica," "sound
   system," "Brixton," and "dub reggae" surfaced mostly: Dutch venue
   (Doornroosje) gig-announcement posters, not photos; UK brewery pub-sign
   photos matching "Brixton" as a place name, not the culture; and
   Natural History Museum specimen sheets matching "sound" or "Don" as
   scientific-name substrings. `reusability=restricted` runs for "reggae
   concert," "Kingston Jamaica street," "Jamaica market," "carnival London,"
   and each Skatalites member name were equally unproductive — mostly
   unrelated in-copyright material or more taxonomy-name collisions (every
   Skatalites-member-name Europeana query hit botanical authorship
   abbreviations, e.g. "G.Don," "D.Don" for botanist Don, nothing to do with
   Don Drummond). "dub reggae" and "Notting Hill Carnival" returned zero
   results outright.

3. **Internet Archive advancedsearch + item metadata — worked mechanically,
   unproductive for photographs.** Returned real JSON with real
   `licenseurl` fields, but IA's full-text search matches keywords anywhere
   in metadata, so nearly every query returned false positives: NASA
   "Honorary awards ceremony" photos for "Dennis Brown" (a NASA-Langley
   staffer, not the singer), a Puerto Rico mayoral-visit photo set for "Rico
   Rodriguez," botanical/genealogy/hobbyist ephemera for most other queries,
   and 45/78rpm record-label-art scans (genuinely reggae-related but sleeve
   art, not photographs of people/places) for "Augustus Pablo," "Lee Perry,"
   "U-Roy," etc. from the "Reggae Label Art Archives" uploader. Beth Lesser's
   "Reggae Quarterly" magazine scans surfaced repeatedly under "Burning
   Spear," "King Tubby," "Sly and Robbie," "Dennis Brown," and "U-Roy" —
   excluded per the skip list (Beth Lesser's own archive is out of scope,
   and these IA uploads carry no `licenseurl` regardless). Queries run:
   Burning Spear, Culture, Dennis Brown, Bunny Wailer, Black Uhuru, Augustus
   Pablo, Sly and Robbie, King Tubby, Lee Perry, U-Roy, Notting Hill
   Carnival, sound system dance Jamaica, Jah Shaka, Saxon Studio, Handsworth
   reggae, Ladbroke Grove reggae, Judge Dread, Laurel Aitken, Rico
   Rodriguez, Desmond Dekker, Israel Vibration, Third World band, Yellowman,
   Scientist dub, Prince Jammy, Joe Gibbs, Bunny Lee, Studio One Kingston,
   Channel One studio, Half Way Tree Kingston, Orange Street Kingston, Tuff
   Gong studio, Black Ark studio, Aswad band, Black Slate band, Cimarons
   band, and the nine Skatalites/Skatalites-member names. Zero usable
   photographs across all of them.

4. **Flickr photo pages — effectively unreachable without a browser.**
   WebFetch can load a Flickr URL, but Flickr's search results and photo
   grids are client-rendered; WebFetch's no-JS fetch returned "no matches"
   for a search that almost certainly has matches, and a profile page
   ("reggaemusicstore," a UK dub/reggae-adjacent account) rendered as "no
   public photos" despite the account showing 95 photos and thumbnails in
   the raw markup. General web search also could not surface specific
   `flickr.com/photos/<id>/<photo-id>` URLs for period reggae subjects —
   `site:flickr.com` queries for Burning Spear/Big Youth/U-Roy/Dennis Brown
   and for Notting Hill Carnival 1978-1981 returned no Flickr links at all;
   a Rotterdam/Amsterdam/Berlin festival query surfaced Flickr URLs but all
   pointed at unrelated or undated modern content. Flickr is confirmed as
   inaccessible through every route available in this environment (matches
   the prior pass's 403 finding, just a different failure mode: the prior
   pass hit a hard block, this pass gets served an empty/unrendered page).

5. **Web search for named photographers / deep-cut subjects — unproductive
   for photographs, useful only for background.** Queries for Dennis Morris
   (the leading reggae/punk photographer of the era) confirmed he exists and
   shot Marley, Perry, and others starting 1973, but returned no licensing
   information — his archive is gallery/commercial, not open-licensed.
   Queries for Misty in Roots/Matumbi/Steel Pulse, sound-system dances,
   Jah Warrior/Steve Mosco's Flickr account, and Rotterdam/Berlin festival
   photography all returned biographical or commercial-stock context
   (Getty, Magnum, gallery shows) with no CC-licensed photograph surfacing.

6. **Wikimedia Commons — used only for the Skatalites, per the owner's
   mid-task override.** This is the one route that worked, and only because
   Commons happens to host a healthy amount of reunion-era Skatalites
   festival photography, self-published by European concertgoers under dual
   CC BY-SA/GFDL licenses with clean per-file metadata (photographer, date,
   dimensions all stated on the file page, not inferred). See section above.
