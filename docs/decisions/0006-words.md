# 6. Words

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Geoff ruled on 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-24 (first in `CLAUDE.md` at commit `68276d9`). Coined
metaphors and record-shop puns crept into early specs and UI copy, and readers had to learn them.

## Decision

The people who share a library are "the group", and one person is "a member". "Family" appears only
in the one-line description of what the product is for. Specs and UI copy use plain terms, with no
coined metaphor (such as "the crate") and no record-shop pun (such as "Back to the shelf"). The
product's identity lives in its visuals, per `docs/specs/2026-09-24-visual-identity-design.md`.

## Consequences

The `web/` reviewers grade the copy register against the visual identity spec.
